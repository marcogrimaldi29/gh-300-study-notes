ⓘ Exam Format

The exam is proctored and may include interactive components. You can explore the exam environment ahead of time using the exam sandbox. A practice assessment is also available. If the exam is not available in your preferred language, you may request an additional 30 minutes.

Skills Measured

The exam covers six skill areas. Note: the official guide treats "Use GitHub Copilot features" (IDE, CLI, capabilities, and org-wide management) as a single 25–30% domain — it's split into the two cards below purely for readability, not because it's two separate domains. Click a card to view the detailed study notes for each area.

Prompt Engineering

Prompt structure, context crafting, zero-shot and few-shot prompting, best practices, prompt process flow.

Privacy & Safeguards
